# Pace Menu: research and editorial decisions

Reviewed 9 November 2026. 32 distinct publications were opened and relevant material read. The two small acute interval experiments were reviewed at abstract level; this is stated in the source register. Coaching pages, manuals, a descriptive framework and original studies answer different questions and are not treated as interchangeable evidence.

## What this review changed

- A curated range of purposes replaces a claim that one session is universally best.
- Race-specific paces stay tied to current equivalent fitness. A race result cannot establish load tolerance, training history or marathon readiness.
- The specialist 1500 m menu uses actual 1500 pace where its source specifies it. Mile-rhythm economy work remains separately labelled.
- Distance recoveries remain distances and their elapsed time is estimated from easy pace. Ratio recoveries scale with the preceding rep. No recovery follows the final repetition.
- Half-marathon pace is distinct from threshold: the event can take less than, close to, or much longer than an hour.
- Full-run simulations include their easy opening and finish; the app does not add them twice.
- Current fitness replaces aspirational goal paces in source examples. Counts, metric rounding and other modifications are disclosed in session details.
- Duration prompts are editorial comparison aids, not physiological laws or personalized clearance. A linked smaller session is offered without silently rewriting the selected workout.
- Demanding simulation examples and predictor guarantees were not adopted as defaults. Lemoncello’s very short-rest simulation was reviewed and excluded; Mahon’s full elite mixed set was not transplanted wholesale.

## Per-session attribution

The application’s `research.ts` maps every fixed session to its source family, explains modifications, and records its intended role and progression. These are research-informed editorial choices, not claims of experimentally proven superiority. Full plans, schedules and coaching prose have not been reproduced.

## Accounting and limitations

Quality running means all non-easy work, including a marathon-pace float inside a half-marathon alternation. Specific-pace distance is tracked separately. Main-set totals include written easy floats. Session time also includes between-rep recovery and any separately specified warm-up/cool-down.

A running-fitness model provides a useful reference, not a laboratory lactate measurement. Weather, terrain, fatigue, event preparation and individual response can require a different effort.
