# Sub-threshold catalogue review

Reviewed 9 November 2026. User clarified the request as individual repetitions up to 5 km. The site contains 35 native sub-threshold choices: 20 timed and 15 distance-based. These are alternatives, not a prescribed progression or a weekly programme.

## Evidence and limits

First-hand Bakken material (2022, 2025, March 2026 and October 2026 interview) supports controlled effort, short practical rests and varied familiar rep lengths. Koens describes conservative starts and a four-by-ten-minute progression. The Talsnes et al. 2024 experiment is an acute uphill crossover in fourteen trained men; it is not proof that any flat-running session is best. No universal optimal repetition count or recovery was established.

The original Sirpoc forum pages were blocked. Attributed reproductions were navigation/context only; their long-session examples were not presented as authenticated prescriptions. Five-kilometre reps and most reduced/expanded doses below are explicit editorial menu adaptations. Long doses are not default recommendations.

## Pacing and workload

Following the pace audit, distance prescriptions choose their family by estimated rep duration (under 5, under 8, or 8+ minutes); timed blocks retain their authored family. These editorial boundaries match the timed menu, not measured physiology. The existing short, medium and long starting bands are retained: estimated one-hour speed plus 5–10, 10–15 and 15–20 sec/km, respectively. These are conservative editorial starting estimates, not a lactate measurement or a formula supplied by a source. Long reps can require slowing further. No fourth physiological zone, universal blood-lactate target, automatic training week or doubles was introduced.

Estimated quality minutes and per-rep duration are calculated at the current fitness. For menu comparison only, more than 45 quality minutes or a repetition longer than 15 minutes is labelled Demanding. These are editorial workload flags, not physiological safety cutoffs or personal readiness decisions. A time-based menu remains one click away. Two five-kilometre reps mean exactly 10 km of work, and three mean 15 km; no rest follows the last rep.

The Minutes / Kilometres selector filters authored sub-threshold formats. It does not silently convert a timed session into a supposedly identical distance session. Km/mi pace and display units remain a separate control. Other training types are unaffected.
