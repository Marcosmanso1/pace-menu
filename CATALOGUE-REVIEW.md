# Session catalogue review

Internal editorial ledger. Source and rationale sections are intentionally absent from the website. Distances are metric-native and are rendered in the selected units; source doses may be rounded or reduced. Current equivalent fitness replaces aspirational pacing. Reviewed 9 November 2026.

## Metric revisions to the retained catalogue

The older imperial marathon split rehearsal is now 3 km easy + 2 × 8 km at marathon pace / 5 min jog + 3 km easy. The older continuous simulation is 8 km easy + 10 km at marathon pace + 2 km easy. These are deliberate rounded metric adaptations, not exact conversions. Source and adaptation notes remain editorial records and are not product sections.
